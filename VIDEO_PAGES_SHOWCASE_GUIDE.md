## 🎥 SCREEN RECORDING BEST PRACTICES

### **DO:**
✅ Use clean test data (no real names, realistic but fake)
✅ Record at 1080p or higher
✅ Use smooth mouse movements
✅ Show cursor only when clicking
✅ Record at 60fps for smooth playback
✅ Test the flow multiple times before final recording
✅ Use consistent timing (don't rush)
✅ Highlight important buttons with zoom or cursor pulse
✅ Show success messages clearly

### **DON'T:**
❌ Don't show real personal data
❌ Don't hesitate or pause during recording
❌ Don't show browser tabs or personal bookmarks
❌ Don't show email or phone notifications
❌ Don't use Lorem Ipsum (use Filipino text)
❌ Don't show empty states (have data populated)
❌ Don't record with low battery indicator
❌ Don't show cursor too much (only when clicking)
